### Fixed

- **Upgrading.** After `brew upgrade --cask chill`, or a new dmg dragged over the old app, chill kept running the old version and the popover asked you to quit and relaunch it. It now relaunches itself into the new version, nothing to press.

### Changed

- **The menu bar fan reads on any menu bar.** While your curve runs it is filled and drawn heavy in chill's own color, darker on a light menu bar and brighter on a dark one; while Apple's curve runs it is an outline, receded but clear.
