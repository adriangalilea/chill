### Added

- **Apple stays in charge of your fans unless you draw otherwise.** chill forces a fan only while you asked for a curve, someone is watching (the app is open, or `chill ... --watch` runs), and nothing vetoes it. Quit the app, lock the screen, let the Mac sleep or run hot, and the fans are Apple's again within seconds.
- **Curves are the whole model.** `apple` is macOS's own. `chill` is a quiet floor, then one clean climb to the fan's maximum, shaped by a single knob. Draw your own point by point on the plot, with the hottest part of the chip and every fan marked live.
- **Honest status.** `chill status` says who holds the fans and why, in one line: your curve, Apple, a veto, no one watching, or another app forcing them. `chill status --json` gives the same for scripts.
- **A menu bar glyph that shows the effect, not the wish:** outline when Apple holds the fans, filled when your curve does. `⌃⌥⌘C` toggles between Apple and your last curve from anywhere.
- **A CLI on the same daemon**: `chill curve use <name> --watch`, `chill boost 5 --watch` for a few minutes at full speed that ends by itself, `chill system` to hand back now, `chill log -f` to watch every decision.
- **One small root daemon, the only thing that writes to the fans.** Install it from the popover and approve it once in System Settings. It never touches the network, and it never stands in the way of sleep.
