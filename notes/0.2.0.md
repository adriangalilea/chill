### Changed

- **chill now needs macOS 27.** On macOS 26, chill 0.1.16 keeps working and gets no further updates.

### Fixed

- **The fans no longer pulse up and down with every burst of work.** A curve now follows the heat in your Mac rather than the chip's reading of the moment, which jumps the second work starts and drops the second it stops. The fans rise within seconds under real load and come down at the pace the Mac cools.

### Added

- **The plot shows what the curve follows.** The live point and its trail move with the heat; a fainter line beside it is the chip's reading right now.
- **`chill sensors`** prints the chip, the heat a curve follows, the ssd, the battery and the fans. `--follow` adds a line every second.

### Performance

- **chill does less work every second.** The app and the fan helper exchange only the live numbers; Apple's observed fan curve, which grew the longer the helper ran, stays with the helper on disk.
