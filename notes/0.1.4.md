### Changed

- **chill runs from the start.** Once its daemon is installed, the `chill` curve takes the fans right away instead of leaving them on Apple's curve until you pick it, and its tab comes first. A Mac that already chose keeps its choice.

### Fixed

- **Uninstalling is just deleting the app.** Delete chill (or `brew uninstall --cask chill`) and its daemon hands the fans to Apple and removes itself within seconds; nothing to run first.
- **The download is notarized itself.** The disk image is signed, notarized and stapled, not only the app inside it.
