### Changed

- **Installing and uninstalling chill are simpler.** chill controls the fans through a small background helper that macOS asks you to allow once. Open chill from the dmg or a download and one press moves it to your Applications folder and installs that helper. If macOS needs your permission, chill names the switch to turn on and opens the System Settings page that has it. To uninstall, drag chill to the Trash: your Mac takes back control of the fans, and chill's settings go to the Trash too, even if chill wasn't running. Installing it again starts fresh.

### Fixed

- **After an update, the new version runs right away.** Before, the old version's fan helper could keep running.
- **The status line no longer breaks off in the middle of a sentence.**
